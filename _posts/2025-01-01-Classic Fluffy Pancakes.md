## Ingredients
- 1 ½ cups (190g) all-purpose flour  
- 3 ½ tsp baking powder  
- 1 tbsp sugar  
- ½ tsp salt  
- 1 ¼ cups (300ml) milk  
- 1 egg  
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
2. In a separate bowl, mix milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla.
3. Combine wet and dry ingredients, stirring until just mixed (don’t overmix!).
4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat. Pour ¼ cup of batter per pancake.
5. Cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook until golden brown.
6. Serve with syrup, fruit, or whipped cream!
