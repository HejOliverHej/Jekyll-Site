## Ingredients
- 1 ½ cups (190g) all-purpose flour  
- 3 ½ tsp baking powder  
- ¼ cup (50g) sugar  
- ½ tsp salt  
- 1 ¼ cups (300ml) milk  
- 1 egg  
- 3 tbsp butter, melted  
- 1 tsp vanilla extract  
- ½ cup (90g) chocolate chips  

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, mix flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
2. In another bowl, whisk milk, egg, butter, and vanilla.
3. Combine wet and dry ingredients, then fold in chocolate chips.
4. Heat a greased pan over medium heat. Pour batter (¼ cup per pancake).
5. Cook until bubbles appear, then flip and cook until golden.
6. Serve with syrup, chocolate drizzle, or whipped cream!
